# ATOMIC BOND

## Master Product Specification --- v0.5

**Status:** APPROVED --- FROZEN BASELINE\
**Supersedes:** Master Product Specification v0.4\
**Working tagline:** **See how connected we already are.**

**Core principle:** Every person is an Atom. Every confirmed human
connection is a Bond. Together, those Bonds reveal a larger human
network.

Atomic Bond exists to make human interconnectedness visible and to
create simple mechanisms through which people can experience the
presence of the larger network around them.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Atomic Bond is a public human-connection network designed to demonstrate
how closely people are connected through chains of real relationships.
Unlike conventional social networks, it is not designed around
followers, popularity, feeds, comments, engagement rankings, or
attention. Its structural unit is a mutually confirmed **Bond**; its
lightweight human-presence signal is a temporary, voluntarily shared
**Pulse**. The core loop is **Connect → Bond → Explore → Pulse → Feel →
Share → Expand**. Agreement is not required for connection.

Global Doodle is a narrowly scoped exception: a moderated, bounded visual-expression
surface available to authenticated ACTIVE participants across Atomic Bond. It does
not require a confirmed Bond and does not authorize a general social-content feed.

---

## 2. ATOM

An Atom represents one participant and receives a permanent public
identifier. Optional public identity may include first name/alias, X
handle, coarse home region, country, and date joined. Sensitive
information is never public. When intentionally explored, each Atom
becomes the visual center of its network. No Atom is inherently more
important than another.

---

## 3. ATOM CREATION

Creating an Atom requires a **verified email address** and **validated
coarse geographic home region**. Alias/name and X handle are optional.
No conventional username/password account is required. Email establishes
private ownership/access. Location must come from a validated geographic
selection, not arbitrary free text. Do not require street address,
precise GPS, phone number, or date of birth.

---

## 4. PUBLIC AND PRIVATE IDENTITY

Public Atom data may include public ID, optional alias, optional X
handle, approved coarse geography, confirmed network information, and
network statistics. Private data includes email, authentication/security
metadata, notification preferences, and precise technical/location
information. **Email is never public.**

---

## 5. X PUBLIC IDENTITY

An Atom may optionally include a normalized validated X handle. Atomic
Bond constructs the outbound X profile URL from the handle and does not
accept an arbitrary URL as the X field. Unless separately verified
later, the product must not imply ownership of the X account has been
verified. X is an optional bridge to an existing public identity. Atomic Bond does not become
a general content-feed platform; the limited Global Doodle exception is defined in
Section 48A.

---

## 6. BOND

A Bond is a mutually confirmed connection between two Atoms. It cannot
be created unilaterally. Only one active confirmed Bond may exist for
the same pair, and only confirmed Bonds participate in the connection
graph.

---

## 7. MOLECULE

A Molecule is the extended network reachable through an Atom's Bonds:
**YOU → direct Bonds → nearby connections → extended connections →
regions**. It is a visualization of connectivity, not a separate
organization or account.

---

## 8. BRIDGE

A Bridge is a Bond connecting previously separate or weakly connected
network regions. Bridge creation is a meaningful network event. Bridge
detection must be deterministic.

---

## 9. CHAIN REACTION

A Chain Reaction occurs when downstream Bonds/invitations continue
propagating without additional action by the original participant,
demonstrating how a small connection can produce larger network effects.

---

## 10. THE LIVING ATOM

The Living Atom is the primary interface. The selected/current Atom sits
at the center of a living spatial system; direct Bonds are most
prominent; distant connections progressively become clusters, regional
structures, and density fields. Conceptually: **ATOM → BONDS → MOLECULE
→ REGIONS → HUMANITY**. It should feel like interconnected solar
systems/molecular structures rather than a conventional network diagram.

---

## 11. SPATIAL EXPERIENCE

Use apparent depth, calm orbital motion, light, particles, scale, and
Bond lines to communicate structure. Direct Bonds should feel personally
close; distant structures should communicate reach without rendering
everyone equally. Scale progresses **PEOPLE → LOCAL → REGIONAL →
COUNTRIES → GLOBAL**. Motion is optional and must respect reduced-motion
preferences.

---

## 12. NETWORK EXPLORATION

Selecting another Atom first reveals context: public ID, optional
alias/X, relationship, coarse region, network reach, and geographic
reach where available. Indirect connection must never imply inherited
trust. **VIEW THEIR NETWORK** is the deliberate action that recenters
the system around that Atom.

---

## 13. MY ATOM

A clearly accessible **MY ATOM** action returns to the user's own
network perspective.

---

## 14. PULSE

Pulse is Atomic Bond's lightweight human-presence mechanism. A
participant voluntarily selects a temporary self-selected Feeling, Energy or Vibe state and sends a
Pulse through actual Bonds. The interaction begins **HOW ARE YOU RIGHT NOW?** and then **SEND PULSE**.

---

## 15. EMOTIONAL PULSE VOCABULARY

**FEELING:** Joyful, Excited, Curious, Calm, Content, Sad, Anxious, Frustrated.

**ENERGY:** Energized, Focused, Motivated, Wired, Tired, Drained, Restless, Lazy.

**VIBE:** Chilling, Hungry, Caffeinated, Tipsy, Pooped, Cozy, Hungover, Under the Weather.

These are everyday non-clinical, explicitly selected states. Categories are taxonomy only; an Atom has one current Pulse across all categories. No free-text state is supported. Existing retired states retain their original meaning until expiry or replacement; they are not remapped by inference. Pulse must never be presented as diagnosis.

## 17. VOLUNTARY EMOTIONAL STATE

Emotions are explicitly user-selected only. Atomic Bond must **never
infer emotion** from browsing, messages, writing, photos, facial
analysis, network behavior, location, activity, AI analysis, or external
social media. No explicit Pulse means no assigned emotion.

---

## 18. TEMPORARY STATE

An Emotional Pulse means **how someone chose to say they felt
recently**, not who they are. It expires automatically after **24
hours** unless replaced. After expiration the Atom returns to neutral. A
temporary state must never become a permanent label.

---

## 19. ONE ACTIVE EMOTIONAL STATE

An Atom may have at most one active Emotional Pulse. A new Pulse
replaces the prior active state. The normal network view uses only the
latest non-expired state. Initial implementation should minimize
unnecessary emotional-history retention.

---

## 20. LIVING EMOTIONAL NETWORK

Authenticated MY ATOM automatically visualizes authorized active Emotional
Pulses across the viewer's connected network. Active Atoms illuminate according
to their selected state; Atoms without an active Pulse remain neutral. This is
the default Living Atom experience, not a separate FEEL THE NETWORK mode.
Public anonymous views do not automatically receive emotional state. Changing
the visual center never expands the authenticated viewer's authorization.
The experience should resemble a living emotional constellation.

---

## 21. EMOTIONAL NETWORK VIEW

The Living Atom shows active emotional Atoms and regional emotional clusters.
A compact, expandable **YOUR NETWORK NOW** information panel shows active-Pulse
count and aggregate distribution, separate from primary actions. Coverage must be
explicit, e.g. **1,842 active Pulses across 18,492 connected Atoms**.
These statistics describe only recent voluntary Pulses from
participating connected Atoms and must not be represented as general
population sentiment. Percentages use only active visible Pulses. The panel
includes available coarse geography and update age, supports zero/small networks,
and updates without requiring a separate emotional-view mode.

---

## 22. EMOTIONAL NETWORK PRIVACY

Emotional Pulse is separate from unrestricted public Atom profile data.
Active Pulse visibility belongs in appropriate connected-network
contexts rather than automatically becoming globally searchable public
profile information. Pulse authorization/visibility must be
architecturally separable from PublicAtom visibility.

---

## 23. REGIONAL EMOTIONAL VIEW

Validated coarse geography may support regional aggregation of active
Pulses. These represent only participating visible Atoms in the viewer's
connected network. Never claim a population or region itself 'is' an
emotion; use contextual wording such as **Among active Pulses in your
connected Florida network...**

---

## 24. EMOTIONAL AGGREGATION

Representation should scale from **individual emotional Atoms → local
clusters → regional distribution → global connected-network
distribution**, rather than rendering every distant Atom individually.

---

## 25. SENDING A PULSE

Flow: **PULSE → HOW ARE YOU RIGHT NOW? → choose FEELING / ENERGY / VIBE → select one state → preview → SEND PULSE**.
Preview is local and neither persisted nor propagated.

The sender's Atom adopts the temporary state; emotional visual energy
propagates through actual graph connectivity. Sending a Pulse does not
overwrite anyone else's emotional state.

---

## 26. PULSE FEEDBACK

After sending, show meaningful calculated reach such as connected Atoms,
cities, regions, and countries reached. Never fabricate reach.

---

## 27. FUTURE RETURN PULSE

Architecture should support future **RETURN PULSE**. A recipient may
respond with **their own** selected emotion; it must not automatically
copy the sender's state. Future feedback may include returns and
geographic/network reach.

---

## 28. PULSE NOTIFICATIONS

Future Pulse notifications may announce meaningful activity but must be
restrained, configurable, and protected against spam. Nonessential Pulse
notifications must be disableable.

---

## 29. EMOTIONAL SAFETY PRINCIPLES

Do not rank emotions. No happiness leaderboard, positivity score,
emotional popularity score, reward for appearing happy, or penalty for
sadness/fear/anxiety/anger. Emotions are signals, not achievements.

---

## 30. NO EMOTIONAL REPUTATION

Do not convert Pulse history into reputation or personality labels such
as '80% happy,' 'frequently angry,' emotional reliability, or positivity
rankings.

---

## 31. CREATE BOND

The primary structural action remains **CREATE BOND**, initiated through
QR, temporary code, secure invitation link, or future proximity
mechanism. Both participants must confirm.

---

## 32. NEW-PERSON BOND FLOW

Core flow: **Existing Atom → CREATE BOND → invitation → new person →
CREATE YOUR ATOM → email → optional alias → optional X → validated
region → email verification → Bond confirmation → BOND CREATED → Living
Atom expands**.

---

## 33. BOND IMPACT

A new Bond should communicate real network impact---direct Bonds, newly
reachable people, cities/regions/countries---rather than merely '+1'.
Values must derive from actual network data.

---

## 34. LOCATION

Location exists for geographic network understanding. Users choose
canonical places from a validated source; arbitrary free text is not
canonical. Atomic Bond does not need to verify legal residency and does
not require exact physical location.

---

## 35. GEOGRAPHIC PRIVACY

Distinguish geography needed for calculations from geography appropriate
for public display. A canonical city may be retained while a broader
region is shown publicly. Exact address and precise user coordinates are
never public.

---

## 36. EMAIL OWNERSHIP

Email provides private Atom ownership/access without requiring
conventional passwords. Secure email links may support verification,
Atom access, Bond notifications, network updates, Pulse notifications,
and recovery.

---

## 37. NETWORK GROWTH EMAILS

Atomic Bond may send meaningful periodic growth summaries. Recurring
updates must be configurable and unsubscribable.

---

## 38. PUBLIC ATOMS

All Atoms have permanent public identifiers and public network
representations. Public pages may include approved identity and
structural information. Emotional Pulse visibility is governed
separately.

---

## 39. SHARING

Every Atom is inherently shareable. **SHARE/GROW MY ATOM** may let
others explore a public Atom and join Atomic Bond.

---

## 40. PROPAGATION

Preserve enough provenance to calculate invitation/Bond propagation,
including direct invitations, downstream participants/Bonds, geographic
reach, and longest chain. These are non-financial growth incentives.

---

## 41. NON-FINANCIAL INCENTIVES

Atomic Bond does not financially reward Bonds or Pulses. Rewards arise
from network expansion, geographic reach, Bridges, Chain Reactions,
human presence, Pulse participation, collective milestones, and visible
connection impact.

---

## 42. GLOBAL NETWORK

Shared statistics may include TOTAL ATOMS, TOTAL BONDS, COUNTRIES
CONNECTED, GLOBAL NETWORK CONNECTIVITY, and AVERAGE SEPARATION.
Emotional statistics must clearly refer only to active voluntary Pulses,
not humanity generally.

---

## 43. CONNECTION REQUESTS

A participant may eventually request an introduction to another
connected Atom. Atomic Bond brokers it without exposing private email.
Recipients may ACCEPT, DECLINE, or BLOCK. Contact details are exchanged
only after mutual consent.

---

## 44. TRUST

Distinguish **Direct Bond** from **Connection through Bonds**.
Connection paths do not imply inherited trust. Never calculate a
generalized trustworthiness score.

---

## 45. ANTI-SPAM

Prevent mass unsolicited messaging and excessive notifications using
appropriate rate limits, cooldowns, blocking, preferences, abuse
reporting, and spam controls. Pulse also requires notification-abuse
protections.

---

## 46. ANTI-FRAUD

Minimum protections include mutual confirmation, unique Bond pairs,
expiring invitations, email verification, server-side validation, rate
limiting, and suspicious-activity detection. Bond/Pulse counts have no
directly redeemable financial value.

---

## 47. THINGS ATOMIC BOND IS NOT

Atomic Bond is deliberately not built around followers, conventional
Likes, comments, popularity rankings, influencer scores, endless feeds,
advertising engagement loops, streak punishment, compulsive
notifications, public emails, precise public locations, emotional
rankings, inferred emotions, psychological profiling, or emotional
reputation.

The only appreciation/ranking exception is attached to individual Global Doodle
drawings under Section 48A. It does not authorize participant popularity profiles,
influence scores, conventional Likes elsewhere, or ranking people or their networks.

---

## 48. CORE INTERFACE

Core interface: **LIVING ATOM** (continuously see the connected network and
authorized active Emotional Pulses); **CREATE BOND** (create a confirmed connection); **PULSE** (share temporary emotion);
**YOUR NETWORK NOW** (expand current authorized emotional-network information);
**EXPLORE** (understand/view another Atom's network); **SHARE** (invite
others).

---

## 48A. GLOBAL DOODLE

Global Doodle lets authenticated ACTIVE Atoms share temporary 64 × 64 monochrome
dot drawings. Drawing, erasing, undo, clear, accessible controls and Light/Dark
presentation are supported. The canvas is the preview; SEND is the final publication
action. Complete bounded replay remains available to authorized moderators, not
ordinary user-facing interfaces. The final bitmap is 512 bytes. No confirmed Bond
is required to create, view, appreciate or report a drawing. Anonymous access is
not part of this feature.

An Atom may have multiple live drawings, each with its own immutable identifier,
publication deadline, appreciations and reports. Publishing another drawing does
not replace earlier drawings. PostgreSQL enforces a minimum of 60
minutes between accepted submissions, including after removal. Retries are
idempotent. Drafts remain local. A valid submission from an authenticated ACTIVE
owner is published immediately after server-side payload/replay validation,
ownership/lifecycle checks, cooldown and request throttling. Ordinary publication
has no pending-review state and requires no administrator approval.

Newly published drawings remain public for exactly 7 days (168 hours) from
successful publication using database time, unless removed earlier for
moderation or account lifecycle reasons. Content
is immutable. The approved editor's bounded-history fallback may submit a still
bitmap with no replay; any submitted replay must be complete within its bounds and
reconstruct the final bitmap. A partial or inconsistent replay is rejected.

Existing published drawings retain their original deadlines.

The Global Doodle page displays “Draw something. Make someone's day.” directly
below its GLOBAL DOODLE heading, using the existing monochrome typography.

Global Doodle has a standalone authenticated ACTIVE-owner page, separate from
the Living Atom and drawing editor. Shared application navigation presents
MY ATOM, GLOBAL DOODLE, ABOUT and SETTINGS in a single horizontally scrollable
row, with an active-page indicator. The Living Atom has no Doodle feed/sidebar.
The LIVE feed defaults to WORLD. MY NETWORK includes the verified owner's own
drawings and those of ACTIVE authors reachable through confirmed direct or
indirect Bonds, using server-authoritative ownership and graph relationships.
Structural inactive nodes may connect the graph but contribute no drawings.
Viewing another Atom never changes this owner-rooted scope. HALL OF FAME remains
global and separate from live-feed ordering. Feed entries show static bitmaps,
permitted public Atom numbers, publication age, APPRECIATE and REPORT. Bounded
cursor pagination and approximately 30-second visibility-aware refresh preserve
scroll position and exclude expired or removed content. Changing scope resets
pagination and clears the old results. CREATE DOODLE opens the existing editor,
which contains no feed or public replay. After server-confirmed publication,
DOODLE PUBLISHED provides RETURN TO MY ATOM and DRAW ANOTHER, disabled until
the authoritative 60-minute submission cooldown ends. Failed submissions preserve
the unsent drawing; retries are idempotent. No additional tracking or private/offline
content cache is introduced.

There is no fixed global daily submission quota. Per-Atom cooldown,
payload/replay bounds and server-enforced request throttling remain authoritative.
Administrators may explicitly pause and resume new submissions globally. Pausing
intake does not hide existing eligible drawings, stop voting, or affect other Atomic
Bond features. An idempotent retry does not republish content or extend its lifetime.

Moderation is reactive. Authorized administrators can inspect active and reported
drawings, including every retained intermediate replay state; remove content
immediately from all public retrieval operations; restrict or restore Doodle posting;
and inspect a private moderation decision record. Review uses bounded cursor pages
without an overall record ceiling. Reports prioritize review but never automatically
remove content solely because of report volume.

APPRECIATE is the sole voting interaction. Each authenticated ACTIVE Atom may
appreciate another Atom's currently published drawing once. Self-voting is
prohibited. No votes are accepted outside its original publication window (7 days / 168 hours for newly published drawings). Retries cannot add votes. There is no dislike, downvote, comment, reply,
repost, follower system, financial reward or transferable reward. The live drawing
shows its appreciation count and whether the viewing owner has appreciated it.

The GLOBAL DOODLE HALL OF FAME holds at most ten read-only drawings. Final scores
are frozen when the original voting window closes. Initial qualification requires
five unique appreciations. Authorized administrators may explicitly change the
threshold to five or higher; it is never automatically lowered. Each drawing uses the threshold
in force at its publication, so later changes do not rewrite eligibility.

Qualification does not itself publish artwork to the Hall of Fame. A qualifying
drawing waits privately for explicit administrator approval for at most seven days
after its live window ends. It then expires if not promoted. At promotion, the
database atomically checks the current gallery. An empty position may be filled;
a full gallery accepts a candidate only if its score is strictly higher than the
lowest incumbent. Equal scores never displace an incumbent. Display ordering is
score descending, promotion time ascending, then drawing identifier ascending.
Among tied lowest incumbents, the newest promotion is displaced first, with the
drawing identifier resolving any remaining tie. Administrator operations cannot
set scores or bypass qualification or capacity rules.

Hall of Fame entries may show rank, drawing, final score, public Atom number and
original publication date. They have no ongoing voting. A displaced entry becomes
unavailable immediately and its payload/replay is deleted by bounded cleanup.
There is no archive of former winners. Artwork remains stored while it occupies
a Hall position, subject to moderation and account lifecycle removal.

Attribution uses only the existing public Atom number; private identity, Auth
identifiers, internal Atom UUIDs, location, preferences and Pulse history are never
part of Doodle display data. Drawings must not be used to infer personal traits.
Participants should not put private personal information into drawings. ACTIVE
participants may report drawings through predefined categories and bounded,
duplicate-protected reporting. Reports alone do not automatically remove content.
Authorized administrators may reject/remove content and restrict Doodle posting.

Deactivation and deletion remove the author's live, qualifying and Hall artwork and
replay. Reactivation does not restore that content. Deactivation retains the
submission cooldown; deletion clears account-linked Doodle state. Votes legitimately
cast before deactivation remain valid. On deletion, private voter links are removed;
open-window counts may consequently decrease. Closed-window scores remain frozen.
Account lifecycle changes never permit new votes from an ineligible account.

Ordinary expired drawing payloads are deleted within 24 hours. Removed, rejected
and displaced artwork becomes inaccessible immediately and is queued for the next
five-minute cleanup, with a one-hour operational removal deadline. Public visibility
never depends on cleanup running. Qualifying private review and
current Hall storage are the explicit retention exceptions. Minimal cooldown and
retry metadata may outlive payloads; it contains no bitmap or replay. Reports/voter
links are removed with their drawing. A minimal private moderation decision log may
remain for up to 30 days, without bitmap/replay or private email; deleted drawing links and
deleted Auth identities are cleared. This log never appears in participant data. Longer retention requires an actual
legal requirement and a separately documented, restricted operational procedure;
this feature introduces no automatic legal hold. No immediate erasure promise is made for
backups or copies already downloaded by another participant.

This exception adds no targeting, notifications, email campaigns, emotional
profiling, changes to Bonds, or broader social-feed authorization.

## 48B. PARTICI-PUZZLE

PARTICI-PUZZLE is an optional shared weekly picture puzzle for verified ACTIVE
Atom owners. It initially uses a 25-piece, 5 × 5 board and administrator-approved
artwork. Administrator-controlled weekly windows do not overlap. Database time
determines the current puzzle; if none is scheduled, show a waiting state.

REVEAL MY PIECE reserves one available piece for five minutes. Each participant
may hold only one reservation. Cancellation or expiration returns the piece and
starts a five-minute reveal cooldown. Revealing does not consume PLACE allowance.

PLACE MY PIECE permits one successful placement per rolling 60 minutes. MOVE A
PIECE permits one successful move per rolling 60 minutes, independently. Both
cooldowns persist across weekly puzzle changes. Incorrectly placed pieces remain
movable and two unlocked pieces may be swapped. Correct placements permanently
lock. Completion requires every piece to occupy its correct position. Failed
actions consume neither gameplay allowance; request throttling still applies.

Authoritative atomic database operations determine ownership, reservation validity,
correctness, locks, deadlines, board revision and allowances. Clients cannot assert
these properties. Participants may see their reserved piece and placed pieces,
but cannot request arbitrary source regions. Full-image delivery is permitted
after authoritative completion or scheduled expiration. Before then, unrevealed
source regions remain inaccessible. Pre-generated private tile bytes are selected by a narrowly authorized PostgreSQL
operation using the existing verified-owner session and database time. Normalized source
artwork is retained privately in PostgreSQL for the same bounded retention period;
source records are not participant-readable. Authorization
and byte selection are serialized with reservation and lifecycle changes. The
application returns private, non-cacheable binary responses; ordinary participants
have no direct Storage access to puzzle media. New retrieval after cancellation or
expiry is denied unless another current rule authorizes that content (for example,
a placed board piece). An already-authorized in-flight response may complete;
previously delivered content cannot be revoked.

Deactivation cancels reservations and prevents participation while preserving
applicable cooldowns and shared progress. Deletion removes participant-linked
private puzzle records and reservations without reversing anonymous shared
progress. Contributor identities are never included in display data.

Puzzle content remains available throughout its scheduled period and for 30 days
after that period closes, including puzzles completed early. Afterward, bounded
cleanup removes source artwork and detailed gameplay records, retaining only
minimal aggregate statistics. Backup retention may delay physical erasure from
recovery copies; restored systems must reapply retention before serving content.

Collective progress may be displayed without individual rankings, rewards or
personal contribution histories retained indefinitely. This feature introduces
no comments, messaging, participant image uploads, votes, push notifications or
changes to Bonds and Pulse. Keyboard/touch operation, Light/Dark presentation and
reduced-motion accessibility are required.

## 49. MVP PRIORITY

Production MVP priorities: Atom creation; email verification; validated
home region; optional alias; optional X; public Atom pages; secure owner
access; mutually confirmed Bonds; Living Atom; exploration/My Atom;
network/geographic reach; sharing/invitations; Emotional Pulse; 24-hour
state; always-on authorized Living Emotional Network; emotional aggregation; regional emotional
visualization; growth email capability; notification preferences.
Anonymous distant introductions and Return Pulse may follow after the
core network is proven.

---

## 50. TECHNICAL FOUNDATION

Preferred foundation: **Next.js/React/TypeScript** frontend; **Vercel**
hosting; **Supabase/PostgreSQL** backend; NotificationService with
**Resend** planned for production email; **Squarespace** for domain/DNS;
Atoms as nodes and Bonds as edges. Keep the Living Atom modular and
database-independent. Use PostgreSQL initially unless measured scale
justifies specialized graph infrastructure.

---

## 51. DETERMINISTIC GRAPH LOGIC

AI must not determine authoritative graph facts. Deterministic
software/database logic determines Bond validity, separation, paths,
reachable counts, Bridge detection, propagation, Pulse reach, and
regional aggregation. AI may assist development but is not the source of
truth.

---

## 52. EMOTIONAL PULSE DATA MODEL

Canonical conceptual model: **EmotionalPulse { id, atomId, emotion,
createdAt, expiresAt }**. The `emotion` identifier represents one approved Feeling, Energy or Vibe state; category is derived from the catalog. Production persistence must enforce/derive a
single latest non-expired active state. Pulse visibility remains
separate from unrestricted PublicAtom serialization.

---

## 53. PULSE DATA MINIMIZATION

Collect only what Emotional Pulse functionality requires. Initial
product does not require explanatory emotion text, inferred causes,
diagnostic labels, or permanent emotional histories. Any future proposal
for historical emotional data requires explicit product/privacy review.

---

## 54. SERVICE BOUNDARIES

Keep UI, Living Atom rendering, graph logic, application services, data
access, authentication, notifications, and database infrastructure
separated. UI must not contain arbitrary database queries. The renderer
consumes generic graph/emotion data and does not own authentication,
email, or authoritative Bond/Pulse persistence.

---

## 55. EMAIL AND NOTIFICATION ARCHITECTURE

Use a replaceable NotificationService. Resend is the planned production
provider, not a product dependency scattered through UI code.
Transactional verification/access email must remain distinct from
configurable recurring growth/Pulse updates.

---

## 56. LOCATION VALIDATION ARCHITECTURE

Use a replaceable LocationService. Production location input should use
validated autocomplete/canonical place records. Store structured
city/region/country/countryCode and, if useful, approximate place
centroid---not user GPS. Editing text after selection invalidates the
canonical selection until a valid place is reselected.

---

## 57. PRIVACY PRINCIPLES

Collect the minimum data necessary. Public/private boundaries must be
explicit and tested. Never expose email, authentication tokens, precise
location, or private security/device identifiers. Emotional state has
its own connected-network visibility boundary.

---

### Explicit account deactivation and permanent deletion

An owner may explicitly deactivate their account reversibly. Deactivation
preserves the private account, profile, permanent Atom number and confirmed
Bonds, while hiding optional public identity and Home Region, removing active
Pulse and suspending engagement email. Transactional access remains available.
Secure owner authentication and explicit reactivation restore the same Atom;
authentication alone does not reactivate it. Preferences remain preserved and
the growth comparison baseline restarts from the current network. Permanent
account deletion remains separate, irreversible and cannot be reactivated.

## 58. SECURITY PRINCIPLES

Security is architectural: server-side authoritative transitions, Row
Level Security where appropriate, expiring invitation/access tokens,
duplicate prevention, authorization tests, sanitized public fields, and
no private fields in public responses. Never weaken security merely to
simplify implementation.

---

## 59. ACCESSIBILITY

Maintain keyboard-accessible controls, semantic labels, readable
contrast, visible focus, screen-reader equivalents for important
metrics/states, and reduced-motion support. Emotion must never be
communicated by color alone.

---

## 60. PERFORMANCE AND SCALE

Do not naïvely render every reachable Atom. Nearby people may be
individual; distant networks should aggregate into
clusters/density/regional structures. Optimize based on measurement.
Keep renderer architecture replaceable (e.g. Canvas today, possible
WebGL later) without rewriting product logic.

---

## 61. AGENT DEVELOPMENT RULES

Agents must read this specification and AGENTS.md before substantial
work; preserve stable functionality; work modularly; add tests; run
regression/security checks; document material changes; never expose
private data; never reinterpret indirect connection as trust; and never
introduce prohibited conventional social mechanics without explicit
specification revision.

## 63. DEFINITION OF PRODUCT SUCCESS

Atomic Bond succeeds when real people create genuine confirmed
connections that make the human network larger, denser, and more
understandable; when participants can see how far their relationships
extend; and when temporary voluntary Pulses create a quiet sense of

## 64. NORTH STAR

Atomic Bond should move the participant from **ME** toward **US**. The
experience should communicate: **I know someone. They know someone. That
person knows someone. We are connected farther than we realize---and,
for a moment, we can feel the presence of that network.**

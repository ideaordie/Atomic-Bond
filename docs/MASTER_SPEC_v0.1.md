# ATOMIC BOND

## Master Product Specification — v0.1

**Working tagline:**
**See how connected we already are.**

**Core principle:**
Every person is an Atom. Every confirmed human connection is a Bond. Together, those Bonds reveal a larger human network.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Atomic Bond is a public human-connection network designed to demonstrate how closely people are connected through chains of real relationships.

Unlike conventional social networks, Atomic Bond is not designed around followers, popularity, content, comments, or attention.

Its fundamental unit is a **mutually confirmed human connection: a Bond.**

The application should encourage people to:

**Connect → Bond → Explore → Share → Expand**

The long-term objective is to create a continuously expanding visualization of human interconnectedness and encourage peaceful cooperation by making those connections visible.

Atomic Bond does not require people to agree with one another.

It demonstrates that people who disagree can nevertheless exist within the same interconnected human network.

---

# 2. CORE OBJECTS

### ATOM

An Atom represents one participant.

Every Atom receives a permanent public identifier.

Example:

**ATOM #00018492**

Every Atom is publicly viewable.

An Atom may optionally contain limited public information such as:

* first name or alias
* approximate region
* country
* date joined

Sensitive personal information is never publicly displayed.

Every Atom becomes the visual center of the network when viewed.

No Atom is inherently more important than another.

---

### BOND

A Bond represents a mutually confirmed relationship between two Atoms.

Example:

**ATOM #102 ↔ ATOM #847**

A Bond cannot be created unilaterally.

Both Atoms must confirm the connection.

Only one active Bond may exist between the same two Atoms.

Bonds form the edges of the Atomic Bond network graph.

---

### MOLECULE

A Molecule represents the extended network reachable through an Atom's Bonds.

Example:

**YOU**

↓

8 direct Bonds

↓

147 second-degree connections

↓

4,891 third-degree connections

↓

184,291 extended connections

The Molecule is not a separate account or organization. It is a visualization of network connectivity.

---

### BRIDGE

A Bridge is a Bond that connects previously separate or weakly connected portions of the Atomic Bond network.

Bridge creation should be treated as a significant network event.

Example:

**BRIDGE CREATED**

*Your new Bond connected two previously distant networks.*

---

### CHAIN REACTION

A Chain Reaction occurs when connections originating downstream from an Atom or Bond continue propagating without additional action by the original participant.

This demonstrates that a small human connection can produce a much larger network effect.

---

# 3. THE LIVING ATOM

The Living Atom is the primary Atomic Bond interface.

The current user/viewed Atom appears at the center.

Direct Bonds form the first surrounding layer.

Second-degree connections appear farther outward.

Additional degrees continue expanding outward.

Conceptually:

**ATOM → BONDS → MOLECULE → NETWORK → HUMANITY**

The visualization should resemble a living atomic/molecular structure rather than a conventional social graph.

It should feel:

* alive
* organic
* scientific
* expansive
* peaceful
* slightly mysterious

The interface should use animation, particles, connections, depth, movement and light rather than conventional profile cards whenever practical.

---

# 4. NETWORK EXPLORATION

Users can select another connected Atom.

When selected, that Atom becomes the new center of the visualization.

The network reorganizes around it.

This reinforces a central Atomic Bond principle:

> **Every Atom is the center from its own perspective and part of everyone else's network.**

Users should be able to move through the network by selecting connected Atoms.

---

# 5. PULSE

Users can activate:

**PULSE**

A visual wave originates from the selected Atom and travels outward through its known Bonds.

The Pulse visually demonstrates degrees of connection.

Example:

**YOU**

→ direct Bonds

→ second degree

→ third degree

→ extended network

Pulse is primarily a visualization and emotional experience rather than a communication mechanism.

---

# 6. CREATING A BOND

The primary action is:

**CREATE BOND**

The production system should support a fast real-world Bond process using mechanisms such as:

* QR code
* temporary Bond code
* proximity mechanism where practical
* invitation link

A Bond request must expire after a limited period.

Both participants must confirm.

Successful confirmation produces:

**⚛ BOND CREATED**

The Living Atom then visually reorganizes to incorporate the new relationship and any newly reachable network.

---

# 7. BOND IMPACT

Creating a Bond should show more than "+1."

Example:

**NEW BOND CREATED**

Previously connected:

**18,492 Atoms**

Now connected:

**27,914 Atoms**

**+9,422 connections revealed**

This makes network effects visible.

---

# 8. PUBLIC ATOMS

All Atoms are publicly viewable.

Each Atom receives a permanent public URL.

Conceptually:

`atomicbond.com/a/00018492`

Anyone may view a public Atom without logging in.

Public Atom information may include:

* Atom number
* optional public name/alias
* direct Bond count
* extended connection count
* degrees of separation
* approximate geographic reach
* countries reached
* network visualization
* propagation statistics

Email addresses are NEVER publicly displayed.

---

# 9. ATOM OWNERSHIP

Atomic Bond should avoid conventional usernames/passwords whenever practical.

The user's verified email establishes ownership/control of their Atom.

Atomic Bond sends secure email links allowing the owner to access management functionality.

A public Atom link provides:

**VIEW ACCESS**

A secure email link provides:

**OWNER ACCESS**

Owner access enables actions such as:

* Create Bond
* confirm Bond
* share Atom
* manage notifications
* manage optional public information
* respond to connection requests

---

# 10. EMAIL

Email functions as both identity recovery and lightweight notification infrastructure.

Atomic Bond should not depend on users remembering passwords.

Emails should primarily be event-driven rather than generic newsletters.

Examples:

**Your Atom grew while you were away.**

**Your network reached another country.**

**Your Atom now connects to 100,000 people.**

**A Bond you created started a Chain Reaction.**

**Your network created a new Bridge.**

**Your Atom doubled in size.**

Each message should contain a secure:

**VIEW MY ATOM**

link.

Email frequency must be restrained.

Users must be able to disable nonessential notifications.

---

# 11. SHARING

Every Atom is inherently shareable.

Primary action:

**GROW MY ATOM**

or

**SHARE**

Sharing generates the public Atom/invitation link.

Recipients should immediately see the inviting Atom and a simplified representation of its network.

Primary invitation:

**BECOME AN ATOM**

Registration friction should be minimal.

---

# 12. PROPAGATION

Atomic Bond should preserve enough provenance to determine how invitations and Bonds propagate through the network.

A participant might see:

**YOUR PROPAGATION**

Direct invitations: **7**

Downstream participants: **1,418**

Downstream Bonds: **3,791**

Countries reached: **18**

Longest chain: **14 Bonds**

These numbers provide non-financial incentives for network growth.

---

# 13. NON-FINANCIAL INCENTIVES

Atomic Bond should NOT financially reward users for generating Bonds.

Primary rewards should include:

* network growth
* geographic reach
* propagation
* Bridges
* Chain Reactions
* connection milestones
* decreasing degrees of separation
* collective global milestones

The primary psychological reward is:

**Seeing something small that you did become something much larger.**

---

# 14. GLOBAL NETWORK

Atomic Bond maintains shared global statistics.

Examples:

**TOTAL ATOMS**

**TOTAL BONDS**

**COUNTRIES CONNECTED**

**GLOBAL NETWORK CONNECTIVITY**

**AVERAGE DEGREES OF SEPARATION**

Eventually Atomic Bond should visually transition from individual nodes into regional/global network density as scale increases.

The ultimate visualization is humanity as one interconnected network.

---

# 15. CONNECTION REQUESTS

A participant may explore outward through their network and select a distant Atom.

They may request an introduction.

Example:

**REQUEST DIRECT BOND**

Atomic Bond sends the request without revealing either participant's email address.

The recipient may:

**ACCEPT**

**DECLINE**

**BLOCK**

No explanation is required.

---

# 16. MUTUAL CONTACT EXCHANGE

Contact information remains private until both participants explicitly agree to exchange it.

Flow:

**Atom A requests connection**

↓

Atomic Bond contacts Atom B

↓

**Atom B accepts**

↓

Atom A confirms contact exchange

↓

**Both approve**

↓

Contact information is exchanged.

Atomic Bond should never expose one participant's email simply because the other participant requested contact.

---

# 17. TRUST

Atomic Bond must distinguish between:

**Direct Bond**

and

**Connection through Bonds**

If:

**A trusts/connects with B**

and

**B connects with C**

Atomic Bond must NOT claim:

**A trusts C.**

Instead:

**A is connected to C through B.**

Atomic Bond visualizes chains of confirmed relationships.

It does not guarantee the character, reliability, safety or trustworthiness of distant participants.

---

# 18. LOCATION

Location exists primarily to visualize geographic network expansion.

Atomic Bond should minimize location data.

Public information should use coarse geography such as:

**City / Region / Country**

or

**Region / Country**

Exact addresses must never be publicly displayed.

Precise historical movement should not become part of the public network.

Atomic Bond should not become a location-surveillance system.

---

# 19. PRIVACY PRINCIPLES

Atomic Bond should collect the minimum information necessary to operate.

Public:

* Atom ID
* Bonds
* optional alias
* coarse geography
* network statistics

Private:

* email
* authentication information
* notification settings
* security/fraud metadata

Never public:

* authentication tokens
* precise location
* private email address
* device/security identifiers

---

# 20. ANTI-SPAM

Anonymous connection requests must be intentionally constrained.

Atomic Bond should prevent mass unsolicited messaging.

Potential mechanisms include:

* limited outstanding requests
* rate limits
* request cooldowns
* recipient blocking
* disabling introductions entirely
* abuse reporting
* automated spam detection

The system should reward meaningful introductions rather than message volume.

---

# 21. ANTI-FRAUD

Atomic Bond should assume that people will eventually attempt to manufacture Bonds.

Minimum protections:

* mutual confirmation
* one Bond per Atom pair
* expiring Bond codes
* rate limiting
* suspicious device/activity detection
* server-side validation
* duplicate detection

Bond counts should have no directly redeemable financial value.

---

# 22. THINGS ATOMIC BOND IS NOT

Atomic Bond is deliberately NOT built around:

* followers
* conventional Likes
* comments
* public popularity scores
* influencer rankings
* endless content feeds
* advertising engagement loops
* daily streak punishment
* compulsive notifications
* public email addresses
* exact public locations

Atomic Bond should not evolve into a conventional social network unless this specification is deliberately revised.

---

# 23. CORE INTERFACE

The primary interface should remain extremely small.

### LIVING ATOM

Primary visualization.

### CREATE BOND

Establish a new human connection.

### PULSE

Visualize connection propagation.

### EXPLORE

Travel through connected Atoms.

### SHARE

Invite another person into Atomic Bond.

Everything else should remain secondary.

---

# 24. MVP

Atomic Bond v1 does NOT need every long-term feature.

Initial production MVP should contain:

1. Atom creation
2. email verification
3. public Atom pages
4. secure Atom-owner links
5. mutual Bond creation
6. Living Atom visualization
7. Pulse
8. network exploration
9. basic connection statistics
10. sharing/invitations
11. coarse geographic reach
12. basic email growth notifications

Anonymous distant introductions may follow after the core network is proven.

---

# 25. TECHNICAL FOUNDATION

Initial preferred architecture:

**Frontend**

Mobile-first web application.

**Database**

Supabase / PostgreSQL.

**Authentication**

Email verification / secure magic-link ownership.

**Hosting**

Vercel.

**DNS / edge services**

Cloudflare where useful.

**Network representation**

Atoms = nodes.

Bonds = edges.

The initial graph should remain in PostgreSQL unless scale demonstrates the need for specialized graph infrastructure.

---

# 26. AGENT DEVELOPMENT RULES

Development agents working on Atomic Bond must:

1. Read this specification before making architectural or behavioral changes.
2. Preserve stable functionality unless explicitly instructed otherwise.
3. Work in modular components rather than repeatedly replacing the entire application.
4. Create tests for new critical functionality.
5. Run existing regression tests before declaring work complete.
6. Never expose private email or authentication information.
7. Never reinterpret indirect connection as verified trust.
8. Never introduce conventional social-media mechanics without explicit approval.
9. Maintain database migrations rather than manually changing production structure.
10. Maintain version history and changelog.

---

# 27. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Atomic Bond should make an enormous network feel personal.

The participant should begin with:

**ME**

and gradually discover:

**US**

The experience should communicate:

> **I know someone.
> They know someone.
> That person knows someone.
> And eventually, we reach almost everyone.**

---

# 28. NORTH STAR

Atomic Bond's success is not measured primarily by time spent in the application.

It is measured by:

**Real people creating genuine connections that make the human network larger, denser and more interconnected.**

The application succeeds when someone opens their Atom and realizes:

> **I am connected to far more of humanity than I thought.**
